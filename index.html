# Scholarship Journey Automation (MPOWER Global Citizen Scholarship)

An autonomous, human-in-the-loop pipeline built for Claude Cowork that automates 8 of the 9 manual steps in the MPOWER Global Citizen Scholarship evaluation cycle, cutting operational turnaround from weeks to days and reducing manual workload by ~95%.

## Overview & Architecture

Each scholarship cycle receives thousands of international student essay submissions answering:
"Tell us how your international education will empower your positive impact, whether locally or globally."

Previously, reviewing submissions required manual coordination across spreadsheets, email threads, evaluation rubrics, document requests, and certificate generation. This skill standardizes and automates the entire lifecycle through a structured 9-stage pipeline:

Pipeline Architecture:
[1. Intake] -> [2. Grammar Gate] -> [3. Batch Scoring] -> [4. Top 10 Shortlist]
                                                               ↓
[9. Cheque Disbursement] <- [8. Certificates] <- [7. Documents] <- [6. Winner Outreach] <- [5. Claire Selects 3]

## The 9 Pipeline Stages

### Stage 1: Intake
- Reads applicant rows from the live Google Sheets submission form via the Google Sheets API.
- Extracts applicant profile: Full Name, Email, Country of Origin, Target University, Degree Program, Funding Gap ($), and Essay Text (500-700 words).
- Validates field completeness and normalizes phone/email formats.

### Stage 2: Grammar & Eligibility Gate
- Performs automated pre-screening for eligibility:
  - Validates enrollment at an eligible U.S. or Canadian institution.
  - Enforces word count thresholds (400-800 words).
  - Flags automated AI plagiarism markers or coherence failures.
- Non-qualifying entries are archived with audit reason; qualifying entries proceed to Stage 3.

### Stage 3: Batch Rubric Scoring (AI Evaluator)
- Evaluates qualifying essays using Claude against a 4-dimensional evaluation rubric (Total: 40 points):
  1. Impact Potential (10 pts): Direct benefit to local community or global challenges; quantifiable outcomes.
  2. Academic & Career Alignment (10 pts): How well the chosen degree program enables the stated mission.
  3. Feasibility & Execution Plan (10 pts): Concrete roadmap, realistic milestones, and demonstrated commitment.
  4. Resilience & Adversity (10 pts): Overcoming financial, socio-economic, or geographic barriers.
- For every applicant, Claude outputs:
  - Numerical scores per category (1-10)
  - Composite Score (weighted 0-100%)
  - 2-sentence executive summary
  - Key standout quote

### Stage 4: Top 10 Shortlist Generation
- Sorts candidate pool by composite score and diversity parameters (geographic diversity across LATAM, Sub-Saharan Africa, South/Southeast Asia).
- Formats top 10 finalists into the "Shortlist" tab of the Google Sheet with clickable links to full essays and synthesized rationale.
- Updates the live Dashboard status to "Awaiting Claire's Selection".

### Stage 5: Human-in-the-Loop Selection ("Claire Selects 3")
- A dedicated review checkpoint where scholarship director (Claire) evaluates the Top 10 dossiers and selects the final Grand Prize ($10,000) and Honorable Mention ($3,000) winners.
- The pipeline pauses until the 3 selections are confirmed in the control sheet.

### Stage 6: Winner Outreach
- Drafts personalized congratulatory emails via Gmail API to the 3 selected recipients.
- Sends warm, encouraging updates to non-winning finalists offering MPOWER credit-building resources and future application waivers.

### Stage 7: Document Verification
- Automatically issues secure upload requests for required verification items:
  - Official university offer letter / proof of enrollment
  - Student visa (F-1 / J-1 / Study Permit)
  - Government-issued photo ID
- Tracks verification status in real-time.

### Stage 8: Certificate Generation
- Generates high-resolution branded award certificates (PDF) with unique verification IDs and digital signatures.
- Attaches certificates directly to winner email confirmation threads.

### Stage 9: Cheque Disbursement
- Prepares finance disbursement package (recipient banking wire details, scholarship reference code, currency transfer schedule).
- Triggers notification to treasury team for fund disbursement directly to university financial aid offices.

The Challenge: Scaling international student lending required rapidly testing objection-handling hooks (no cosigner, fixed rates, visa letters, career support) across India, Africa, and Southeast Asia. Traditional creative workflows took 5–7 days per multi-slide carousel through external design queues, bottlenecking paid social performance.

The AI Solution: Built an automated creative pipeline integrating ChatGPT Codex, the Canva Connector API, and Slack. Codex transforms raw value-proposition briefs into structured JSON schemas with slide-by-slide narrative arcs (Hook → Core Offer → Ecosystem Support → Final CTA). The Canva Connector programmatically populates pre-approved brand templates (applying MPOWER colors, fonts, and assets), and posts a 1-click preview in Slack for marketing review and instant 1080×1350px export.
